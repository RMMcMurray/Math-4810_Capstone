Data Science Capstone: Commissioner’s Office House Price Analysis

The Problem

  • Property values are only captured at the point of sale
  • Between sales, assessors must estimate value manually — no data-driven basis
  • Estimates are inconsistently applied and difficult to defend in court
  • The result: property tax assessments that are arbitrary and legally vulnerable

Our Approach

  • Phase 1 — Understanding the Market: Identify what drives property value and uncover natural sub-markets in the data
  • Phase 2 — Building the Model: Predict property value from numerical and non-numerical features, adjusted for market trends
  • Phase 3 — The Application: A tool for assessors to input property features and receive a defensible, data-backed value estimate

Model Selection & Strategy

We discussed three distinct architectures to determine which could best model the Iron County real estate.

  1. Elastic Net: A hybrid of Lasso and Ridge regression. It handles it handles overlapping variables by penalizing redundant variables. It is excellent for transparency, but struggles with the “curvy” non-linear nature of modern market shifts.

  2. Random Forest Trains 100+ independent decision trees on random subsets of data and averages their “votes.” It captures feature fnteractions. It understands that a “3rd Garage Bay” adds more value to a luxury ranch than to a high-density efficiency unit.

  3. Gradient Boosting Trees are built sequentially. Each new tree specifically targets the “errors” of the previous one to minimize the Mean Absolute Error. Gradient Boosting is very precise. It pushes the \(R^2\) to its mathematical limit by refining the model’s focus on outliers.

Vision & Architecture

Deployment direction discussed in our most recent stakeholder meeting:

  • Beta Version: Scoped to the Commissioner’s Office only, preserving data confidentiality without requiring public-facing infrastructure.
  • Future Version: The office may explore opening access to the public, which would require dedicated data centers and formal security protocols to protect sensitive parcel data.
