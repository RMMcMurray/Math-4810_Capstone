Quick Thoughts

Most of the boolean data is unbalanced a lot of the data is dratically skewed to the right. This makes sense beacause mansions dwarf regular houses. As far as heatmap goes the prices are all highly correlated. Anything with the word sqft is correlated with price. Bedrooms and bathrooms correlat with price. Rooms, bathrooms, and sqft all correlate with each other.
